# Terminology

Application-owned interface text is English. Keep receiving-system/database/API values unchanged when integrations depend on them; map them only for display.

Approved shared vocabulary includes: Dashboard, Proposal, Activity, Trainings, Directory, Analytics, Profile, LMS, Simple, Ongoing, Completed, Upcoming, Public, New Teacher, and Trainer.

`Public` as a participant type is not the same concept as the `Public Training` category. Do not globally replace the latter.

Do not fabricate hierarchy such as Lead Trainer, Senior Trainer, Head Trainer, or Primary Trainer unless the receiving system genuinely persists and authorizes that business role. Assigned Trainers are presented neutrally; `You` may identify the signed-in Trainer.

Do not automatically translate stored course/session titles, names, proposals, feedback, descriptions, filenames, uploaded content, or other user/business data.
